## 2. Selection Rationale for Phase U1 Proof-of-Concept Quest: Antyayordashake'pi

For the initial platform validation in Phase U1, **Antyayordashake'pi** (`ANTYAYORDASHAKEPI`) is chosen because:
1. **Direct Foundation Alignment**: It extends World 1's *Ekadhikena Purvena* ($n \times (n+1) \,\|\, 25$) to any two numbers sharing the same tens prefix whose unit digits sum to 10 ($T \times (T+1) \,\|\, U_1 \times U_2$).
2. **Deterministic & Rigorous**: Exact integer arithmetic with zero floating-point ambiguities or deferred irrational cases.
3. **High Pedagogical Delight**: Immediately intuitive and rewarding for players unlocking their first side-quest after World 1.
