# Upa-Sutra Treasury: Master Documentation & Registry

**Project:** *Ankh: The Sutra Saga*  
**Scope:** The 13 Vedic Upa-Sutras (Correlative Sub-Sutras)  
**System Status:** Phase U5 Consolidated & 100% Verified  

---

## 1. Overview & Architecture

While the **16-World Main Campaign** teaches the 16 primary Vedic Mathematics Sutras through a progressive storyline across ancient Gurukuls, the **Upa-Sutra Treasury** serves as an expansive companion treasury:
- **Treasury Side Quests**: 9 rich, standalone playable side-quests unlocked by mastering their corresponding parent worlds in the main campaign. Each quest includes Guru/Disciple story beats, step-by-step guided examples, 5 guided practice problems with hints, 3 timed boss challenge problems, and reward ceremonies.
- **Upa-Sutra Codex**: A comprehensive, scrollable compendium of all 13 canonical Upa-Sutras, detailing Sanskrit aphorisms, historical origins, worked examples, and mathematical proofs.

> **Pedagogical Invariant**: All 13 canonical Upa-Sutras are represented in the Codex. Only methods that provide a distinct, non-trivial, non-duplicate player skill are built as standalone interactive Treasury quests. Redundant or degenerate mathematical cases ($x=0$ equations or duplicate coefficient checks) are preserved as high-quality Codex references.
