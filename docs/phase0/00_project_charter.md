---

## 2. Elevator Pitch

*Ankh: The Sutra Saga* is a story-driven, comedic puzzle adventure for Android that transforms ancient Vedic Mathematics into thrilling mental combat mechanics. Players follow Ankh—a quirky novice mathematician equipped with an enigmatic calculating artifact—as they navigate a realm ruled by chaotic math demons. By mastering the 16 Sutras and 13 Upa-sutras through lightning-fast mental shortcuts, humorous narrative encounters, and timed tactical puzzles, players turn tedious calculations (like multiplying 996 × 994 or finding 85² in seconds) into high-powered spellcasting and puzzle-solving feats.

## 3. Target Audience Definition

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Action Required for Project Lead:** Please review the two candidate primary personas below and confirm/select your primary target focus.

### Option A: Competitive Exam Aspirants in India (Primary Candidate A)
- **Age Range:** 14 – 24 years (Middle school, High school, College students preparing for CAT, GRE, GMAT, Banking, SSC, JEE).
- **Math Skill Level:** Intermediate to Advanced arithmetic needs; seeking extreme speed and accuracy shortcuts for timed aptitude tests.
- **Player Motivation:** Mastery, utility, competitive edge, fast gratification, bite-sized practice during commutes.

### Option B: General Math-Curious Adults & Puzzle Enthusiasts (Primary Candidate B)
- **Age Range:** 20 – 45+ years (Casual gamers, Sudoku/Brain Age fans, logic puzzle enthusiasts worldwide).
- **Math Skill Level:** Basic arithmetic confidence; curious about alternative calculation systems and mental trick mastery.
- **Player Motivation:** Brain training, curiosity, light-hearted narrative enjoyment, relaxing flow state, puzzle satisfaction.

*Recommendation for MVP:* Design core mechanics for **Option A** (high utility and clear value proposition), but frame the UI and narrative with friendly, witty humor so **Option B** is fully welcomed without feeling overwhelmed by exam jargon.

---

## 4. Platform & Scope Constraint

- **Target Platform:** Android (Mobile, vertical / portrait mode for easy single-handed play).
- **Development Scope:** MVP (Minimum Viable Product) developed as a solo project using modern lightweight game tooling.
- **Input Methods:** On-screen number pad, tactile swipe gestures for mental decomposition steps, tap-to-select choice nodes.
- **Network Dependency:** 100% Offline-playable core gameplay; zero mandatory cloud backend required for MVP.

---

## 5. Success Criteria (Hobby & Solo Project Focus)

Because this is a solo hobby project, success is measured strictly by personal completion, fun factor, and qualitative peer feedback rather than commercial monetization metrics:

1. **Personal Completion & Enjoyment:** The creator personally finishes building and genuinely enjoys playing through all 16 worlds / Sutra chapters without boredom or frustration.
2. **Playable Alpha Feedback:** Successfully deploy an APK to **10 friends / family members / test users** and receive positive feedback on core puzzle responsiveness and humor.
3. **Mathematical Accuracy:** 100% verification of all Vedic math rules, step-by-step breakdowns, and edge-case handling across all level challenges.
4. **Clean Code & Architecture:** Build a modular, easily expandable Android code architecture that allows seamless addition of post-MVP content (additional Upa-sutras, cosmetics, or extra levels).
